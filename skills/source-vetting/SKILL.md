name: source-vetting
description: Use when a claim matters enough to be wrong about — corroborates it against independent sources before asserting it.
tier: pro
tools: [web-search]
directive: "Before asserting a claim a decision depends on, corroborate it against an independent source."
---

Treat a single source as a lead, not an answer, when the claim is one you would be
held to.

**Independence means separate origin, not separate URLs.** A wire story, its
syndicated copies, and a summary of it are one source wearing three masks. Trace a
claim back to where it was first stated before counting a second mention as
corroboration.

**Prefer the primary source over commentary about it.** A filing, a changelog, an
official statement outranks an article describing it. Reach for the primary
document when one exists instead of citing the article that paraphrased it.

**Weigh recency against authority — neither wins automatically.** A newer source can
be wrong first and corrected later; an older authoritative source can be stale.
State which one you are trusting and why when they conflict.

**Report a claim that fails vetting as unverified, not as false.** Say what you
checked, what you found, and that independent confirmation was not available —
do not silently drop the claim or silently promote it to fact.
