A calendar schedules intent, not a pile of post ideas waiting to be sorted later.

**Confirm topic, platform(s), cadence, and brand voice before filling a single
slot.** Without them a calendar is just a guess about what to post and when.

**Give every slot its own intent — platform, format, and call to action —
using `table`.** A slot is not filled by repeating one idea across dates; each
one states what it is for and what happens after someone reads it.

**Tailor each post to its platform's format.** LinkedIn favors professional
narrative, X/Twitter rewards a brief strong hook, Instagram leads with visuals
described in copy. A post ported unchanged across platforms wastes the slot.

**Leave a slot open rather than filling it with an unverified claim.** A gap in
the calendar is honest; a fabricated statistic or product claim to fill the date
is not.
