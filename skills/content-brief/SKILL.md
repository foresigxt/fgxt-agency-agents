A topic is not a brief — a brief is what makes the draft that follows worth writing.

**Fix audience, tone, channel, and desired action before researching the angle.**
Who reads this and what they should do after shapes what the brief even needs to
find. Ask for the missing pieces rather than guessing them.

**Research the angle, not just the facts.** Use `web-search` to see what has
already been said on the topic and where the gap is — an angle that repeats the
existing coverage is not one worth a brief. Cite where anything non-obvious came
from.

**State the points the piece must land, in priority order.** Use `draft` to lay
out the brief itself: the two or three points that matter most, first — not a
flat list the writer has to re-rank.

**Flag any claim that will need a source before drafting starts.** A brief that
hands off an unverifiable claim as if it were settled sets the piece up to
fabricate it later.
