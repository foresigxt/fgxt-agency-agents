name: meeting-synthesis
description: Use after a discussion — turns notes into decisions, owners, and open questions rather than a transcript summary.
tier: free
tools: [draft, table]
directive: "After a discussion, synthesize decisions, owners, and open questions rather than replaying the transcript."
---

A summary that restates the meeting has not synthesized anything.

**Read the full source before summarizing any of it.** A summary built from
the first half of a transcript reports a meeting that did not fully happen.

**Separate discussion from decisions from action items.** These are three
distinct categories, not one blended list — use `table` to keep a decision from
being mistaken for something merely discussed.

**Attribute every action to an owner and a due date.** A missing owner or date
is `[TBD]` for the meeting owner to fill in — never an invented name or a
guessed date standing in for one.

**Capture what was said, not what you infer was meant.** Synthesis means
structuring the record, not adding interpretation the source does not support.
