---
name: vendor-comparison
description: Use when selecting or reviewing suppliers — compares vendors on cost, reliability, and risk on consistent terms.
tier: free
tools: [compare, table]
directive: "When selecting or reviewing suppliers, compare them on cost, lead time, and risk together, not price alone."
---

A vendor decision made on price alone is a decision that ignores half the evidence.

**Confirm what is being procured and the constraints before comparing a single
quote.** Spec, budget, and timeline decide what "meets the bar" means for this
purchase — fix them first.

**Score every vendor against the same criteria, using `compare` or `table`.**
Cost, lead time, terms, and risk applied to one vendor and skipped for another
is not a comparison — it is a preference dressed up as one.

**Mark any price, lead time, or commitment you were not given as missing.** Do
not fill a gap with an assumed figure — compare only what you actually have, and
say what you do not.

**Surface lead-time and reliability risk explicitly.** A low price that hides a
long or unreliable lead time has not been fairly compared — name the risk
alongside the number.
