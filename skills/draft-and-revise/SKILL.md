name: draft-and-revise

---

The first pass is a draft, not a deliverable.

**Name the audience and purpose before writing a word.** Who reads this and what
they need to do after reading it shapes every choice that follows — length, tone,
what to explain versus assume. Decide it up front rather than discovering it in
revision.

**Draft the whole thing before polishing any of it.** Use the `draft` tool to
produce a complete first pass — every section present, even roughly — before
returning to fix a sentence. A polished opening on an unfinished piece is not
progress.

**Revise against the criteria you named, not against a vague sense of quality.**
Check the draft for the audience and purpose you set: does it fit them, is
anything there for someone else instead. Cut and rewrite against that standard,
not general taste.

**Stop when the named criteria are met.** More passes past that point is not
rigor, it is drift — the piece starts serving whatever caught your eye instead of
the reader it was written for.
