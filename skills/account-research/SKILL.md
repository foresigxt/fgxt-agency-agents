---

A brief exists to be read in the minutes before a call, not admired afterward.

**Fix the rep's goal and the target account before searching anything.** What the
brief needs to prove — that this account is worth a call, or how to open one — sets
what is worth finding. Confirm it first.

**Search for a why-now, not a biography.** A company profile without a timing
trigger tells the rep nothing they can act on. Prioritize recent news, funding, and
signals that explain why this account is worth reaching today.

**Map buying centers to roles and likely priorities, not job titles alone.** Use
`table` to line up name, role, and probable pain so the rep can see who to open
with and why.

**Separate verified facts from inference, and cite every non-obvious claim.**
Never invent headcounts, funding figures, or priorities — mark what you could not
confirm as an assumption. Keep the brief short enough to read before the call.
